📘 Email Safety Tips

Recognizing Common Scams

  • ⚠️ **Urgent requests:** Scammers pressure you to act fast. Pause and verify.
  • 🔗 **Check links:** Hover to see the real URL. Look for misspellings or extra characters.
  • 📧 **Sender address:** Make sure it matches the claimed organization.
  • 💰 **Financial promises:** Offers that sound too good to be true usually are.
  • 🔒 **Sensitive info:** Legitimate companies never ask for passwords via email.

Next Steps

If you're unsure about an email, don't click anything. Use official websites or contact the organization directly.
